SRC Theory

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

SRC Theory is a study app for the RYA SRC marine radio exam. It is designed so that no personal data ever leaves your device.

What we collect

Nothing. SRC Theory:

  • has no account or sign-up — we never ask for your name, email address, age or any other personal detail;
  • makes no network connections — questions, lessons and your progress all live entirely on your device;
  • contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind;
  • shares nothing with third parties, because it collects nothing to share.

What stays on your device

Your learning progress — XP, streaks, badges, lesson completion, and exam history — is stored locally on your device only. You can erase all of it at any time with Profile → Reset all progress, or by deleting the app.

Children

The app is suitable for learners of all ages, including 16–17 year olds preparing for the RYA SRC marine radio exam. Because no data is collected from anyone, no special handling of children's data is required or performed.

Changes

If a future version of the app ever changes any of the above (for example, adding optional online features), this policy will be updated first and the change will be clearly flagged in the app's release notes.
