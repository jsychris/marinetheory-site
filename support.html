Frequently asked questions

Is this an official RYA or Ofcom app?

No. SRC Theory is an independent study aid. It isn't made by or affiliated with the RYA, Ofcom or any training centre, and no RYA course material is reproduced. Questions and lessons are original, written from publicly documented procedures: MGN 324, Ofcom ship radio licence terms and the international VHF channel plan (ITU RR Appendix 18).

Does it cover the practical part of the exam?

No — the real SRC assessment includes a practical session on a VHF/DSC set (routine calls and simulated distress procedures) as well as a written paper. This app covers the knowledge; practise the hands-on radio work on your RYA course.

How closely do the mock exams match the real paper?

The RYA doesn't publish the SRC paper's format or marking scheme, so the mocks don't claim to replicate it. Instead they hold you to a deliberately tough standard: the Full Paper is 30 questions in 30 minutes with a pass mark of 80%. If you pass here comfortably, the knowledge side of the real assessment should feel manageable.

Do I need an internet connection or an account?

No to both. Everything works offline, there's no sign-up, and the app has no adverts or tracking.

Will I lose my progress if I change phone?

Your progress is stored only on your device, so it isn't synced between devices. It is kept in your normal iPhone or iPad backup, so restoring a backup to a new device should bring it with you.

How do I reset my progress?

Go to Log → Clear the log and confirm. This clears your XP, streaks, badges, completed lessons and exam history. It can't be undone.

How up to date is the content?

The content reflects UK marine VHF procedure and licensing guidance (MGN 324, Ofcom licence terms) as of 2026. Channel allocations and licensing details do change, so always check rya.org.uk and ofcom.org.uk too. If you think something is wrong or out of date, please email us.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 or later.
